Doubled the size of every battle map: the attribute grid grows from 160x128 to 320x256 cells (about 5.1k x 4.1k world units), with matching orbit-ceiling, fog, and water-tessellation rescaling in the renderer. Weapon ranges are unchanged, so guns now reach well under half the map and HQs only take aimed fire on deep push-ins.
