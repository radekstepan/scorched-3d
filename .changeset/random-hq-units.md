HQ reinforcements are now random with strength-paced build times: each live HQ draws its next unit weighted by inverse strength (Peewees most often, Hammers rarely) and rearms its clock for that unit's own build time (30 s for a Peewee up to 70 s for a Hammer), replacing the fixed Peewee → Flash → Stump → Hammer cycle on a flat 40 s clock.
